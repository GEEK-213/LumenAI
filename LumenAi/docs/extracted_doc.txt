1. Analysis  
With the increasing demand for intelligent and efficient learning tools, AI -powered 
educational applications are becoming essential in modern academic environments. This 
project aims to design and develop an educational support application titled Lumen AI:  
Illuminate Your Learning. The application is specifically designed for students and focuses 
on automating the note -taking process by allowing users to upload documents or record 
lecture audio, which is then securely stored and organized into structured da ta. Lumen AI 
integrates an AI chatbot that enables students to retrieve information and receive instant 
answers whenever needed. Additionally, the platform enhances learning through 
automated flashcards and quizzes, promoting better retention and long -term 
understanding. Available on Android devices, Lumen AI provides a professional, 
innovative solution that simplifies information management while supporting students in 
achieving academic success.  
 
1.1 Proposed System  
Artificial intelligence is transforming the way students manage and interact with academic 
content. Lumen AI  is a comprehensive educational support application designed to 
automate and enhance the note -taking process. The platform allows students to log in, add 
their classes, select specific lecture dates, and upload audio recordings or documents from 
their sessi ons. The system then generates accurate transcripts along with fully summarized 
and structured notes, enabling students to clearly understand compl ex topics without 
spending hours manually organizing information.  
 
The application includes intelligent features such as an AI -powered chatbot that can 
answer questions based strictly on the recorded class content. Students can select a specific 
lecture date and request detailed explanations, clarifications, or key points  instantly. In 
addition, Lumen AI automatically generates flashcards and quizzes to test understanding 
and support exam preparation, reinforcing knowledge retention through active recall and 
structured practice.  
 
Overall, Lumen AI enhances educational experiences by providing a streamlined and user -
friendly platform for academic organization and revision. With reliable long -term storage 
powered by Supabase and an intuitive Android -based interface, the application p rioritizes 
ease of use and efficiency. By automating note -taking and content structuring, Lumen AI

1.1.1 Objectives  
The Lumen AI: Illuminate Your Learning application is designed with several key 
objectives in mind, each focused on improving academic efficiency and reducing the 
pressures students face in modern educational environments:  
1. Enhance Academic Efficiency and Reduce Stress:  
Our primary goal is to minimize the time students spend on manual note -taking by 
automating lecture transcription and summarization. By streamlining this process, Lumen 
AI allows students to focus on deeper understanding of subject material while reducing 
academic stress and improving overall exam performance.  
2. Automate and Structure Learning Materials:  
The application aims to transform unstructured lecture audio and documents into 
organized, structured notes. By enabling students to upload recordings or files based on 
specific classes and dates, Lumen AI ensures that information is clearly categorized, e asily 
accessible, and academically meaningful.  
3. Provide Instantaneous Academic Support:  
An AI -powered chatbot is integrated within the platform to function as a virtual academic 
assistant. This feature allows students to ask detailed questions related to specific lectures 
and receive accurate responses based solely on their recorded content, fostering an 
efficient and responsive learning environment.  
4. Strengthen Exam Preparation and Knowledge Retention:  
The application automatically generates flashcards and quizzes designed to test 
understanding and reinforce key concepts. This objective supports active recall practices, 
helping students prepare more effectively for assessments and long -term knowledge 
retention.  
5. Support Diverse Educational Levels:  
Lumen AI is designed to accommodate learners ranging from high school students to 
university and even PhD candidates. By providing structured academic assistance across 
various subjects and complexity levels, the platform promotes independent and adaptable  
learning.  
6. Expand Accessibility and Future Integration:  
Currently developed as a prototype and offered free of charge, Lumen AI aims to expand 
its accessibility by launching on major app stores and developing a web -based platform for 
laptop users. Future integration with platforms such as Google Classroom is en visioned to 
enhance usability and ensure seamless adoption within existing educational ecosystems.

 1.1.2 Features  
The Lumen AI application introduces a focused and practical set of core features designed 
to streamline academic workflows and enhance productivity for students. Each 
functionality has been carefully developed to automate essential study tasks while 
mainta ining simplicity and efficiency within the Android platform. Below is a detailed 
overview of its primary features and capabilities.  
1. Automated Lecture Processing and Smart Note Generation  
Lumen AI supports multiple file formats, including documents and audio files such as 
PDF, DOCX, and MP3. After a user uploads lecture material and selects the appropriate 
class and date, the system processes the content and generates a raw results page 
containing both a full transcript (for audio recordings) and an AI -generated summary. This 
automation significantly reduces the time students spend manually writing notes. While 
summaries are initially  structured around main topics, students can further refine or 
reorganize content through interaction with the chatbot, enabling clearer academic 
organization.  
2. Context -Based AI Chatbot Assistance  
The application integrates an AI chatbot designed to answer questions strictly based on the 
uploaded and processed lecture materials. Students can select specific class dates and 
request clarification on recorded content, ensuring responses remain relevant  and context -
specific. Although the chatbot currently focuses on direct question -and-answer 
functionality without advanced adaptive learning features, it serves as an immediate 
academic reference tool, reducing the ne ed to manually search through notes.  
3. Automated Flashcards and Multiple -Choice Quiz System  
To strengthen exam preparation and knowledge retention, Lumen AI automatically 
generates flashcards and multiple -choice quizzes derived from processed lecture content. 
Flashcards are system -generated and designed to reinforce key concepts through active 
recall techniques. The quiz feature provides multiple -choice questions and displays scores 
upon completion, enabling students to assess their understanding before examinations. 
While flashcards are not currently editable, the automated system ensures consistency and 
structured revision support.  
4. Academic Organization and Class Management System  
Lumen AI allows users to create and manage their own subjects or classes based on 
individual academic needs. Students can assign uploaded materials to specific classes and 
dates, helping maintain chronological organization. Although filtering options are 
currently limited to calendar -based navigation and class name search functionality, the 
structure provides a foundational academic management system that centralizes lecture 
materials within one accessibl e platform.  
5. User Interface and System Infrastructure  
Developed as an Android prototype, Lumen AI offers a partially minimal interface with 
dark mode support to enhance usability and visual comfort. The application operates

1.2 Hardware and Software Specification  
 
1.2.1 Hardware Requirements  
1. Development Environment:  
The development of Lumen AI is conducted on systems running the Windows operating 
system, utilizing  VS code  as the primary development environment.  VS code  provides the 
necessary tools for coding, testing, debugging, and performance optimization to ensure 
stable application deployment on supported Android devices.

Lumen AI is designed exclusively for Android devices running Android version 8.0  or

across different device types.  
Device Requirements:  
• Minimum 4GB RAM for stable performance  
• Minimum available storage space below 2GB for installation and operational data  
• Functional microphone access (user permission required) for recording lecture audio  
• Stable internet connectivity for AI processing and data handling

1.2.2 Software Requirements  
 
Flutter  
 
 
 
 
Flutter is an open -source UI software development toolkit developed by Google that 
enables developers to build natively compiled applications from a single codebase. It is 
widely recognized for its ability to create high -performance, visually consistent, a nd 
responsive mobile applications. Flutter uses a rich set of pre -designed widgets that follow 
modern design principles, allowing developers to construct intuitive and aesthetically 
pleasing user interfaces efficiently. Its hot reload feature significantly  accelerates 
development by enabling real -time updates without restarting the application.  
In the Lumen AI application, Flutter is responsible for constructing the entire Android user 
interface, including authentication screens, class management pages, lecture upload 
interfaces, chatbot interaction windows, and quiz modules. Its reactive framewo rk ensures 
smooth transitions between screens and efficient rendering of dynamic content such as 
transcripts and summaries. Additionally, Flutter’s scalable architecture makes it suitable 
for potential future expansion to other platforms, including web and  iOS, without requiring 
a complete rewrite of the codebase.

 Chapter 4  
 
4.1 Conclusion  
The development of Lumen AI represents a significant step toward integrating artificial 
intelligence into everyday academic workflows. This project was designed with a clear 
objective: to reduce student stress, automate time -consuming note -taking processes , and 
enhance overall academic performance. In an era where students are constantly managing 
dense lecture materials, multiple subjects, and limited time, Lumen AI provides a 
structured and intelligent solution that simplifies learning management.  
 
Through out the development process, the application was built to function exclusively on 
Android devices running version 8.0 and above, ensuring compatibility with a wide range 
of modern smartphones and tablets. By leveraging Flutter and Dart for the frontend, th e 
project achieved a responsive and intuitive user interface capable of handling dynamic 
content such as transcripts, summaries, flashcards, and quizzes. The integration of FastAPI 
and Python on the backend enabled efficient communication between the mobil e 
application and AI services, while Supabase provided reliable database management and 
user authentication support.  
 
A key achievement of this project is the successful implementation of automated lecture 
processing. Students can upload audio recordings or academic documents in supported 
formats, after which the system generates full transcripts and concise summaries. Th is 
automation eliminates the need for manual note -taking, allowing learners to focus more on 
understanding concepts rather than transcribi ng information. The AI -powered chatbot 
further strengthens this functionality by enabling students to ask questions based strictly 
on their recorded lectures, ensuring relevant and context -specific responses.  
In conclusion, Lumen AI successfully fulfills  its primary mission of automating academic 
organization and reducing cognitive overload for students across high school, university, 
and postgraduate levels. By combining intelligent automation, structured content 
management, and AI -driven assistance with in a single mobile platform, the project 
demonstrates how technology can meaningfully enhance modern education.

 4.2 Future Goals  
As Lumen AI continues to evolve beyond its prototype stage, several strategic future goals 
have been identified to enhance its functionality, scalability, and overall impact within the 
educational technology landscape. These planned developments aim to str engthen 
accessibility, introduce sustainable monetization strategies, improve personalization, and 
expand platform availability while maintaining the core mission of reducing academic 
stress and automating learning workflows.  
 
1. Multiple languages  and Audio Processing Enhancement  
expand compatibility with multiple languages, particularly in audio processing and 
transcription. Currently, lecture recordings are processed  only with English  however, 
future development will focus on integrating multilingual speech recognition systems. 
This enhancement will allow students from diverse linguistic backgrounds to upload and 
process lectures delivered in various languages. By incorporating multili ngual audio 
transcription and summarization, Lumen AI can broaden its accessibility to internatio nal 
students and institutions. Such expansion not only increases inclusivity but also 
strengthens the application’s potential for global adoption in academic environments.  
 
2. Monetization Through Premium Subscription Plans  
Premium subscription plans as part of a structured monetization model. While Lumen AI 
currently operates without paid features, introducing tiered premium plans would ensure 
long-term sustainability and continued technological improvement. Premium offerings  
may include expanded storage capacity beyond free -tier backend limitations, faster AI 
processing, advanced summarization options, enhanced chatbot functionality, and priority 
access to newly released features.  
 
3. Customized Assessment Generation  
The current system automatically generates multiple -choice quizzes; however, future 
iterations will incorporate fully customized test generation. This feature would allow 
students to select difficulty levels, define specific topics within a lecture, choose  question 
formats such as short -answer or scenario -based questions, and determine the number of 
questions per test.
